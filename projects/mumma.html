MUMMA

10 minutes / Narrative short / Drama

Donna, a newly arrived St. Lucian woman navigates the final weeks of her pregnancy, drawing strength from her grandmother Mumma while longing for her Caribbean roots while weaving a heartfelt tale of resilience, motherhood, and the enduring bond between generations.
